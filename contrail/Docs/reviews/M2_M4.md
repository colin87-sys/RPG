## Paired comparison vs M1 review (6.17)

**This set is better overall than M1 in look (M2 set 6.33 vs 6.17). Juice has not moved and has arguably slipped from 5 to 4.**

Improved:
- **The title is fixed (M1 D-5).** The wordmark is a solid, legible fill, and the capture set is consistent (one run, one timestamp).
- **Global RGB split is mostly fixed (M1 D-3).** The clouds and arches are no longer double-imaged at frame centre. Some residue remains on point lights and at the right edge.
- **Bullet rendering is fixed in Violet and Wreck (M1 D-2).** Rounds there are single discs with a dark ring, as on the board, with a sensible size of about 3-4% of frame height.
- **The TGT panel is now live (M1 D-1, partly fixed).** It shows STRIDER / BULWARK with HP and no longer contradicts the screen in Cloudgate and Wreck.
- **Two new stages exist and match their boards.** Violet Tide and Wreckfield give the game its bright-to-dark range, and the BULWARK boss is on screen.
- **The hull reads near-dark as the bible specifies,** and the HUD scrim is in place.

Unchanged or regressed:
- **Enemy readability is not fixed (M1 D-1).** There are still no lock brackets, the Cloudgate enemy is hidden, and no shooter is visible in Violet.
- **The Cloudgate coin stack persists (M1 D-2).** Rounds fired toward the camera still merge into a column over the reticle.
- **Smoke is now absent rather than thin (M1 D-4).** The missile counter shows 0/6 in every frame, so no missiles appear in any capture and smoke cannot be judged. Treat this as a regression in evidence.
- **Player fire and speed streaks are still missing,** and "N CHAIN" still duplicates the STREAK cell.

## Top 5 defects (ranked by visual impact)

### D-1 — Boss peak has no spectacle: no beam, explosions, ring or smoke   [impact: high]
Capture: /home/user/RPG/contrail/Docs/captures/m2/wreck_combat.png (also wreck_vista.png)
Expected: The bible's §5 effects language and "Current values" should all appear in the peak frame. That means a beam (white core at 55-65% width, warm edge, strong red halo, telegraph line first), explosions with a white-yellow core and orange lobes, 3-band RGB shock rings, and persistent cream missile smoke. Ref 07 relationship: at the peak, effects take up a large share of the frame.
Actual: The only effects are two static rows of 11 hostile rounds and the boss's lit emitters. There is no beam or telegraph, no hit flash on the boss, no bursts or rings, and no smoke. The ARMS cell shows missiles 0/6.
Suggested fix: Script the capture moment (or the boss pattern) so the M4 frame lands during a BULWARK beam volley, with a telegraph plus at least one firing beam. Include at least one player missile salvo with smoke visible (start the capture with missiles > 0), and boss hit flashes and damage pops from player fire. Add one explosion plus shock ring from a destroyed escort or asteroid in the same frame.
Owner lane: vfx (beam/explosion/ring/smoke) + Integrator (boss pattern timing, capture script)

### D-2 — Hostiles hidden or absent; no lock brackets in any combat frame   [impact: high]
Capture: /home/user/RPG/contrail/Docs/captures/m2/violet_combat.png (also cloudgate_combat.png)
Expected: Per the hud board, every on-screen hostile gets red square-corner brackets, a health sliver and a lock count. Per bible §4/§7, enemies are dark with a warm rim and bright markers, readable at play distance.
Actual: In Violet, five rounds are inbound and no enemy is visible anywhere (TGT SCANNING / CONTACTS 01). In Cloudgate, the strider (≈ 880,450) is a small dark shape behind its own bullet column, with no brackets. Wreck shows no brackets on the boss or its sub-parts either.
Suggested fix: Draw passive red corner brackets on every hostile inside the frustum. When a shooter is off-screen, draw an edge-of-frame arrow chevron. Keep engaging enemies at 2.5% of frame height or more, and give Violet enemies the thin warm rim so they separate from the indigo sky.
Owner lane: HUD + enemies (Integrator for the off-screen indicator wiring)

### D-3 — Cloudgate bullet streams collapse into "coin" columns over the reticle   [impact: high]
Capture: /home/user/RPG/contrail/Docs/captures/m2/cloudgate_combat.png (also cloudgate_vista.png, cloudgate_hud.png)
Expected: Per vfx-bullets B, each round reads as a separate disc with a white-hot core and a dark ring, even in dense clusters.
Actual: About 10 rounds fired toward the camera overlap into one diagonal orange column (≈ 850-1060, 470-560). It hides the enemy that fired them and covers the reticle. Wreck and Violet rounds render correctly, so this is a problem with the pattern or spacing, not the sprite.
Suggested fix: Space streams fired toward the camera with a time gap and a small lateral spread, so rounds are at least one disc apart on screen. Alternatively, fade and shrink rounds farther than about 120 m and give only the leading round full size. Aim patterns so the stream does not align with the camera axis.
Owner lane: enemies (fire patterns) + vfx (bullets)

### D-4 — Boss framed small, flat-on and symmetric; no scale drama   [impact: med]
Capture: /home/user/RPG/contrail/Docs/captures/m2/wreck_combat.png
Expected: Bible KEEP: "big boss silhouettes". Ref 07 relationship: the capital ship is seen from below or at an angle, overhangs the camera and fills most of the frame. Enemies board B shows BULWARK from below and behind.
Actual: BULWARK sits dead centre at about 35% of frame width, level with the camera. The rows of rounds run parallel to its wing, and asteroids cross in front of its hull, so the peak reads as a flat diagram.
Suggested fix: For the boss phase, drop the rail camera about 15-25 m below the boss with a 10-20° up-pitch and a slight roll. Bring the boss close enough to span at least 60% of frame width, and keep a clear asteroid-free cone between the camera and the boss's centre. Fan the volleys so they converge in perspective.
Owner lane: Integrator (rail camera / boss staging) + structures (asteroid exclusion zone)

### D-5 — RGB-split residue on hull point lights and frame-edge arches   [impact: low]
Capture: /home/user/RPG/contrail/Docs/captures/m2/cloudgate_hero.png (also cloudgate_combat.png ≈ 1850,250; wreck_combat.png asteroid edges)
Expected: Per bible §5 and post board A: RGB split is mild and confined to the frame edges, and the rainbow effect is reserved for shock rings.
Actual: Every small hull light on the right-hand hull shows a red/green/blue triplet (hero ≈ 1300-1900, 50-250). The top-right arch edge and the wreck asteroid edges (≈ 120-440, 830-940) have visible colour fringes, which reads as noise on the hull's detail lights.
Suggested fix: Clamp the split strength to 0 inside about 70% radius and to 1.5 px or less at the corners. Alternatively, apply the split before the emissive-lights pass, so the point lights stay registered.
Owner lane: post
